# The Archives

> **The record hall of The Realm.**

The Archives preserves the history, evidence, and visual record of the infrastructure work across The Realm. It is the place for screenshots, diagrams, exported results, and other artifacts that help explain what was built, what changed, and what was learned.

## Purpose

The Archives exists to keep evidence discoverable without confusing evidence with live configuration. Materials stored here should support the repositories that produced them and should be understandable when viewed later, outside the original troubleshooting session.

Archive material may include:

- Screenshots and diagrams
- Sanitized command output
- Before-and-after evidence from changes
- Historical project artifacts
- References to the repository and system that produced an artifact

Do not store passwords, private keys, tokens, unredacted logs, or other sensitive data here. Sanitize evidence before it enters the archive.

## Evolution

The Archives will grow from a collection of retained artifacts into a deliberate evidence system for The Realm. Future improvements may include consistent naming, repository and date metadata, links back to the originating work, and clear separation between current evidence and historical material.

The Archives records the journey; it does not replace the documentation, configuration, or operational ownership held by the other repositories.
